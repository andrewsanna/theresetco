6 Smart Home Products That Genuinely Make Life Easier (And Calmer)

By Anna Andrews, Founder of The Reset Co.

I started The Reset Co. for one reason: to help people feel less stressed in their own homes. And while most of my work is physical — decluttering, organizing, building systems that actually stick — I've learned over the years that a calm home isn't just about what you can see. It's also about what you no longer have to think about.

That's where smart home technology comes in. I'm not someone who chases gadgets or upgrades for the sake of it. Every product in my home has to earn its place. But over the last several years, a handful of smart home finds have quietly become non-negotiable for me — not because they're flashy, but because they remove friction. They automate the small, repetitive things so my brain isn't cluttered with them. And that mental quiet? It adds up.

Here are six products I actually use and genuinely stand behind.

1. Philips Hue Smart Bulbs

I've been collecting these for five years. My strategy: I never pay full price. Every Black Friday, I add a few more to the house — a lamp here, an overhead fixture there — until eventually, almost every light in my home was on the system.

The result? I haven't touched a light switch in years. My lights turn on in the morning and turn off at night, automatically, based on whatever schedule I've set. I don't think about it. I don't remember to do it. It just happens.

There's an upfront cost here, and I won't pretend otherwise. But spread out over years of Black Friday sales, it's been very manageable — and the payoff in daily ease is real. If you want to start small, pick one room. The bedroom or living room is usually a good place to begin.

2. Smart Plugs

If the Hue bulbs feel like too big of a commitment right now, this is your entry point — and honestly, it delivers nearly the same idea for a fraction of the price.

A three-pack of smart plugs runs about twenty dollars. You plug them into any standard outlet, connect them to an app, and suddenly anything plugged into them is on a schedule. Lamps, fans, a coffee maker that starts brewing before you get out of bed. The concept is identical to a fully automated lighting system — you set it, you forget it, and it runs quietly in the background.

I'd recommend starting here if you're newer to smart home products. It's low risk, immediately useful, and it'll show you exactly why automation is worth it.

3. Roomba

This one's simple, so I'll keep it simple: I set my Roomba on a nightly schedule, and I wake up every morning to clean floors. I don't think about it. I don't schedule it in my head. I don't feel guilty about skipping it. It happens whether I remember or not.

For anyone who's ever gone to bed thinking "I really should vacuum tomorrow" and then didn't — this is the fix. It's one of those purchases that sounds indulgent until you have it, and then it becomes completely impossible to justify not having it.

4. Rechargeable Motion-Sensor Under-Cabinet Lights

This might be the most underrated product on this list. No electrician, no wiring, no installation beyond sticking them up. They charge via USB and turn on automatically when they detect motion.

I have them in my bathroom and my kitchen, and they're genuinely one of my favorite things in my house. At two in the morning when you need a glass of water or you're up with a kid, you don't have to flip on every overhead light and shock yourself awake. The light comes on when you walk in and turns itself off when you leave.

They're inexpensive, they're easy, and they solve a problem you didn't know was bothering you until it's gone.

5. Skylight Calendar

This one isn't just for you — it's for your whole household.

The Skylight Calendar is a dedicated screen that displays your family's shared schedule. It syncs with Google Calendar, it's always visible, and it means everyone in the house is looking at the same information at the same time. No more "I didn't see that text" or "I thought that was next weekend." The schedule lives on the wall, in plain sight, updated in real time.

If you have kids, a partner with a packed schedule, or anyone else whose plans intersect with yours daily, this is worth every penny. It removes an entire category of household miscommunication — and if you've ever had a stressful scramble because of a missed appointment or a scheduling overlap, you know exactly how much that's worth.

6. Amazon Alexa

Full transparency: I didn't want one. I thought it was unnecessary, maybe a little gimmicky, definitely not something I needed. Then I got one as a gift, put it in my kitchen, and now I genuinely don't know what I'd do without it.

I love to cook — really cook, multiple dishes, multiple timers, hands constantly covered in something. Alexa has become my kitchen co-pilot. I set timers by talking. I convert measurements out loud. I add things to my grocery list while my hands are full. I ask questions without stopping what I'm doing.

If you spend any real time in your kitchen, you'll use this every single day. That's not a guess — that's what happened to me, and I was skeptical going in.

The Bigger Picture

None of these products are about having a smart home for the sake of it. Some of them have a real upfront cost, and I respect that — I've been building this list slowly and intentionally over years.

But here's what I know after doing this work professionally: clutter isn't only physical. Mental clutter — the constant low-level hum of things you need to remember, decisions you need to make, tasks you're behind on — weighs just as much. Probably more. Anything that automates a small, repetitive part of your day is giving you something back. A little more quiet. A little more presence.

That's why I started The Reset Co. Not to make homes look a certain way, but to help people actually feel better in them. These six products do exactly that.
